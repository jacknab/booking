You've identified something real. Every salon SaaS is a feature brochure. Nobody is actually mining the data sitting in their own system to tell a salon owner "here's the money you're leaving on the table and here's exactly how to get it back."

Here's what that looks like as a real product:

The core problem you've spotted: Salons don't lose clients dramatically — they lose them quietly. A client who came every 5 weeks just... stops. The salon has no idea until it's too late. That silent drift is where the real revenue loss is.

Here's what I'd build on top of what you already have:

1. Client Drift Engine (biggest direct revenue impact)
Every client develops a visit cadence — every 4 weeks, 6 weeks, whatever. The system learns it. When someone goes 20% past their personal cadence without rebooking, it automatically fires a personalized SMS: "Hi Sarah, it's been 7 weeks since your balayage — time for a refresh? Book now: [link]" — not a blast, a personal nudge at exactly the right moment. This alone can recover 10-15% of quietly drifting clients.

2. Revenue Leakage Report
Monthly, cold hard numbers: "You had 45 active clients in March. 8 of them haven't booked since. Estimated lost annual revenue: $3,200. Here are their names. One tap to reach them." Not a dashboard stat — an action item.

3. Dead Seat Intelligence
Tuesday 2-4pm is consistently 20% booked. The system finds that pattern, identifies clients who've booked Tuesdays before and haven't been in 6+ weeks, and gives the owner a one-click "fill those seats" campaign.

4. No-Show Prediction
Score every upcoming appointment: booking lead time, client no-show history, day of week, service type. Flag the high-risk ones the morning before. The owner sees "3 appointments tomorrow have elevated no-show risk" and fires confirmations with one tap.

5. Rebooking Rate by Stylist
"Ashley rebooks 78% of her color clients. Jake rebooks 39%." That number is sitting in your data right now. Surface it. Tell Jake what's different. This changes behavior.

6. Smart Cancellation Recovery
When a cancellation hits, immediately scan: active waitlist + lapsed clients at-cadence + clients who've taken that same service + available that time. Fire a targeted "opening just came up" text to the top 3. Turns cancellations from dead revenue into filled seats.

7. LTV + Churn Risk Score
Every client gets two numbers: lifetime value and churn risk. High LTV + rising churn risk = the most dangerous combination. Surface those clients prominently. "Your 8 most valuable at-risk clients represent $11,400 in annual revenue."

8. One Growth Score
A single business health number (0-100) on the dashboard home screen composed of: retention rate, rebooking rate, seat utilization, average ticket trend, new client conversion. When it moves, it tells you exactly why.
