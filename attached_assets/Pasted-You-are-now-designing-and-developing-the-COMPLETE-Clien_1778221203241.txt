====================================================
BUSINESS MODEL
====================================================

Certxa SalonOS uses STAFF/SEAT-BASED BILLING.

Pricing:
- $8 per active staff seat

Examples:
- 1 staff = $8/month
- 5 staff = $40/month
- 12 staff = $96/month

IMPORTANT:
There are NO traditional plan upgrades/downgrades.

Instead:
- Increasing staff count = subscription increase
- Reducing staff count = subscription decrease

The billing system must treat staff quantity as the subscription quantity.

Stripe subscriptions should use:
- quantity-based billing
- Stripe subscription item quantity updates
